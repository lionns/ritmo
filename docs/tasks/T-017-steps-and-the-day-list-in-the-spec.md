  requirement for the day mark, stating it carries no hour, no duration and no condition, and that

  six; the day mark is a state of `/`, and the full step list stays inside the project.

  app still asks for a trigger, and the owner keeps using the version that made them stop.
- §6 carries a real effect that nothing here measures. §1 and §12 support what replaces it, but the
  combination was not tested as a package in any work this project cites. `D-024` names that.
- The step list is the failure mode `brief.md` § Users describes. The three guardrails are the only
  thing between this and the plan that rots — which is why they are scope, not decoration.
- `FR-20` is the quiet one: the requirement most likely to be left pointing at a dead field.
